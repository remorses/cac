**Migrate Template: Content Migration Tool**

Migrate Template automates content transfer to new Framer templates. One credit migrates one word of content. An average landing page contains 500 words.

**How Credits Work**

**1.** 1 credit = 1 word migrated
**2.** Unused credits roll over to the next billing cycle
**3.** Credits remain valid throughout active subscription

**Efficiency Comparison**

Manual migration of a 500-word page:
**1.** Time per revision: 1.5 hours
**2.** Typical revisions needed: 3
**3.** Total time: 4.5 hours
**4.** Cost (at $60/hour): $270 per page

Migrate Template:
**1.** Migration time: 1 minute
**2.** Human intervention: Minimal
**3.** Time saved: 4.4 hours
**4.** Money saved: $264
